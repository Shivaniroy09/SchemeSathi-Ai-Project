Problem Statement

The ₹3+ Lakh Crore Welfare Delivery Crisis

India allocates massive welfare budgets, yet millions remain excluded due to 4 systemic failures:

1. Massive Information Asymmetry
Over 500+ Central and State welfare programs exist across 50+ ministries. Citizens have no unified, simple way to check where they qualify without complex legal gazettes.
2. Predatory Middlemen & "Dalal" Culture
Unregistered agents charge poor citizens ₹500–₹2,000 just to fill free government forms, siphoning off benefits before they even reach beneficiaries.
3. Privacy Violations & Phishing Fraud
Unverified third-party websites harvest Aadhaar cards, mobile numbers, and bank details under the guise of scheme registration, exposing citizens to cyber financial fraud.
4. Technical & Language Friction
Official government portals are heavily fragmented, frequently undergo URL reorganizations, and lack intuitive, responsive mobile design for grassroots users.
The Solution

Zero-Friction Civic Empowerment

Designed from first principles to be fast, honest, and accessible to everyone in Bharat.

1. Zero KYC Needed
Zero server logging, zero database tracking. Citizens test their criteria freely without surveillance anxiety.
2. Bilingual UX
Fluid toggle between English and Hindi with simple, dignified civic iconography and terminology.
3. Explainable Matches
Shows clear match percentage with explicit criteria pills (e.g. "Age 18-40 satisfied", "Income bracket met").
4. Unblockable Links
Uses standard native links with rel="noopener noreferrer" to ensure zero browser blocking or pop-up errors.

Sequence Flow

Component Sequence & Lifecycle

Chronological step execution inside the client application:

1. Bootstrap & Caching
Citizen opens SchemeSaathi. Netlify CDN responds in <50ms. PwaRegister initializes sw.js, caching core assets and fonts into CacheStorage.
2. State & Evaluation
Citizen fills the 4-step wizard. React state triggers calculateEligibilityScore() locally. Weighted scoring assigns 0-100% confidence.
3. Dispatch & Direct Routing
User selects scheme card. Native <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"> anchor opens official portal directly in fresh tab.
AI & Algorithms

Algorithmic Intelligence & Explainable AI

Where AI/Algorithms are used in SchemeSaathi and why client-side execution matters:

Where It Operates: lib/eligibility.ts
  • Multi-Factor Weighted Scoring: Computes compatibility vectors using Category (30%), Income ceiling (25%), Age range (20%), State jurisdiction (15%), and Gender criteria (10%).
  • Zero-Knowledge Evaluation: Citizen inputs are never transmitted to cloud LLM APIs, preventing personal leakages.
  • Explainable AI Matching: Users see exactly why they qualified (e.g. "Satisfies income < ₹2.5L bracket").
Future Indic AI Roadmap
  • On-Device Voice AI: Integrating Bhashini / Whisper Indic models for Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Telugu voice queries.
  • Semantic Scheme Search: Vector embeddings for search by citizen natural language (e.g. "I want loan for small tea shop").
  • Document Guidance Assistant: Conversational AI explaining required paperwork before applying.

Backend Infrastructure

Zero-Server Serverless Edge Architecture

How SchemeSaathi runs with 99.99% availability and zero backend maintenance:

SSG Static Site Generation
  • Configured with output: 'export' in next.config.ts
  • Pre-compiles all HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and JSON during build
  • Removes Node.js server vulnerability surface completely
  • Zero cold starts, sub-50ms Time To First Byte (TTFB) worldwide
Netlify Edge & Security Headers
  • public/_redirects: Ensures SPA client routing with /* /index.html 200
  • netlify.toml: Implements X-Frame-Options: DENY, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
  • Content Security Policy & Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS)
  • Zero hosting cost overhead for civic open-source operation
