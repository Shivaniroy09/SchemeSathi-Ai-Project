Problem Statement

•
₹3+ Lakh Crore Last-Mile Welfare Asymmetry: Over 1,000+ government welfare programs exist, yet 40%+ of intended low-income and rural beneficiaries remain completely unaware due to fragmented ministerial portals and legalistic gazettes.
•
The Predatory 'Dalal' (Middleman) Trap: Rural and low-literacy citizens frequently pay ₹500–₹2,000 or 10%–30% in illegal cuts to informal agents just to discover schemes or fill basic application forms.
•
Severe Cyber Security & Identity Theft Vulnerabilities: Unofficial aggregator apps harvest sensitive Aadhaar cards, bank account details, and biometrics on unencrypted servers, exposing vulnerable citizens to digital financial fraud.
•
Digital Access & Mobile Friction: Official government websites are desktop-centric, poorly optimized for smartphones, and experience frequent server crashes or timeouts during peak application cycles.
•
Research & Engineering Challenge Addressed in MC400: How to formulate a zero-document, privacy-preserving, mobile-first civic discovery platform that empowers citizens to transparently evaluate welfare eligibility and access authentic government endpoints with zero intermediary dependence.

Workflow (DFD & ERD)

LEVEL 0 CONTEXT DFD (Data Flow Diagram)
• Citizen (Client / PWA): Interacts on mobile/desktop; inputs demographic profile without KYC.
• Inbound Stream: Age, State, Caste/Category, Annual Income, Occupation.
• 0.0 SchemeSaathi Engine: Local zero-knowledge evaluation in transient RAM.
• Outbound Deliverables: Ranked cards, match %, missing rules, direct .gov.in links.
LEVEL 1 FUNCTIONAL DFD (Modular Flow)
• 1.0 Profile Intake: Client-side sanitization into strongly-typed UserProfile schema.
• 2.0 Rules Engine: Evaluates criteria against Schemes Store [D1].
• 3.0 Weighted Ranking: Score S = Σ(w_i * c_i) & descending sorting.
• 4.0 UI State & Compare: Reactive cards & side-by-side comparison matrix.
• 5.0 Portal Dispatcher: Sanitized native links (rel='noopener') to official portals.
ENTITY-RELATIONSHIP DATA MODEL (ERD)
• Entity: SCHEME (Knowledge Store):
PK id (string) | name, nameHi, department, ministry, categoryId (FK), level ('Central'|'State'), state, minAge, maxAge, genders[], socialCategories[], incomeLimit, benefits[], howToApply[], applicationUrl, verificationStatus
• Entity: CATEGORY (Taxonomy):
PK id (string) | nameEn, nameHi, icon, schemeCount, color
• Entity: USER_PROFILE (Ephemeral Client Entity):
age, gender, state, caste, annualIncome, occupation, isStudent, isDisability (Zero DB Storage)
• Entity: MATCH_RESULT (Dynamic Evaluation):
schemeId (FK), matchScore (0–100%), isEligible, matchedRules[], missingCriteria[]
• Cardinality: CATEGORY (1) —< (N) SCHEME | USER_PROFILE (1) — Evaluated —< (N) SCHEME => (N) MATCH_RESULT

Methodologies

•
Software Engineering Methodology: Iterative Agile Scrum framework with bi-weekly development sprints, strict static type validation, and continuous deployment via Netlify Edge CDN.
•
Multi-Factor Weighted Scoring Heuristic (lib/eligibility.ts): Statutory scoring model: Match Score = [ Σ(w_i * c_i) / Σ(w_i) ] * 100%, calibrated by statutory rejection risk:
• Caste / Social Category (Weight: 30%): Absolute statutory eligibility barrier (SC, ST, OBC, EWS, General). Incompatibility immediately flags ineligibility.
• Annual Household Income (Weight: 25%): Means-tested statutory income ceiling (e.g. ₹2.5L for scholarships, ₹8L for EWS).
• Age Bracket & Qualification (Weight: 20%): Verifies chronological boundaries [minAge, maxAge] against target welfare criteria.
• Geographic Jurisdiction & State Domicile (Weight: 15%): Differentiates Central universal programs from State-specific entitlement schemes.
• Target Beneficiary Incentives (Weight: 10%): Applies affirmative weighting for girl child, persons with disabilities (Divyangjan), and students.
•
Zero-Knowledge Runtime Execution Model: Demographics are evaluated strictly in transient browser RAM (O(N) time complexity). No personal data packets ever leave the client device.
